VI. Derived Statistics
HP Generation Mode
Hero: lower half of die rolled, upper half guaranteed (e.g., d8 becomes d4+4). Random: full die per level. Maximum: take the die value (no roll). The very first level always takes the max die regardless of mode.
T/ACtical Combat — Equipment AC Modifiers
Per HH p.347. Armor and Shield contribute to Total AC. Natural AC adds to Critical-confirmation tier and also grants +5 HP and +1 natural hardness per point. Same-type natural-armor sources don't stack — best wins.

▸ ⓘ How This Page Works — click to expand

The top of the page is your ledger and combat dashboard. Wealth tracks your funds — every item added below is either 💰 Purchased (deducted at the market-adjusted DC) or 🎁 Acquired free. Equipped — Held & Worn shows what is where on your body right now (hands, armor, and a two-handed weapon's claim on the off hand), and Equipped Weapons — Attack & Damage is the live dashboard: attack and damage lines computed from your stats, proficiencies, mastercraft and enchantments, with alternate modes (autofire, flurry, Rapid Shot…) alongside. Magic / Imbued Item Body Slots maps the body-slot affinities — enchanted items land in their natural slot as you add them.

Below that sit the inventories: Enchanted & Imbued Items (browse the full catalogue), Weapons, Armor & Shields, and Tools & Skill Kits — add from the pickers, then Equip/Unequip on each row; the ▸ beside an item's name opens its dossier — provenance, Purchase DC, and the ✨ Enchant / Mastercraft controls (the result becomes the name: “+2 Mastercrafted (Damage) Plasma Pistol”); ✎ renames an item, 💱 sells it at another market's prices. (Owning a tool kit is enough to use it — no equipping needed.) At the bottom is the motor pool — Vehicles, Mecha (samples, or 🏗 custom builds), and Starships (🚀 commissioned through the shipyard). They are bought here but operated on the 🕹 Vehicles tab, which appears once you own something pilotable.

This page serves two tabs over one surface — Arcane / Divine Spells and Psionic / Super Powers — each showing its own side of your repertoire. The header carries your pool chips: power points and effective level (EAL/EDL for spells, EPL for powers, ESL for a super-mutant) per pool, with per-class breakdowns. Known Spells & Powers is the repertoire itself — the Cast/Manifest button spends PP from the right pool and opens the cast-options dialog (augmentation steppers, metamagic and Power feats, Overchannel and kin where they apply); the numbers land in the blue resolution panel, which stays up until you dismiss it.

Prepared casters (Wizard, Cleric, Ranger) get the Prepared Spells panel: PP are committed at dawn when memorizing and casting a prepared spell is then free — the Tracker's ☀ Fully Rested re-memorizes for the new day. Below, the ⌕ catalogue toggle opens the full corpus with search and filters ("castable by this character" respects your lists, levels, and discipline gates — ⚠ entries are GM-confirm advisories). Super-mutants also see their MP-purchased powers and the momentary-focus flow on the Powers side.

▸ ⓘ How This Page Works — click to expand

The panels track play-time resources. Hit Points heal and damage by amount, with ⚙ HP Mode for generation modes and re-rolls — HP recovery is always manual, because resting conditions can limit it. Action Points spend here and reset only at level-up, never on rest; Fate Point controls appear for Hero-classed characters in Heroic 5+ scenarios. Power Point Recovery's ☀ Fully Rested starts the new day: every PP pool refills, prepared lists re-memorize, per-diem class abilities restore (weekly abilities keep their count) — and it deliberately touches neither HP nor AP.

Experience Points: + Award, − Spend (mastercraft, item creation, and XP-cost spells or powers — the floor rule stops you spending below your current level), and ⬆ Level Up. Conditions toggles the common states with the full list behind More…, and shows the active mechanical effects beneath. Armor Integrity and Per-Diem Abilities panels appear only when you have something to track. At the bottom, Action History is the structured AP/FP audit trail, and the Session Journal is your free-text story log.
